Privacy Policy for Puzzle Of Figures

Last updated November 15, 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Puzzle Of Figures, a free Android game developed by Hamlet Oniki Hovhannisyan, publicly listed as HAMLET HOVHANNISYAN. The game has no advertising, in-app purchases, or player accounts.

For privacy questions or requests, contact gh16games@gmail.com.

Game progress and settings

The game saves your level progress, language preference, and music and sound settings in its app storage on your Android device. It uses this information to restore your progress and preferences when you play again.

Depending on your device settings, Android may include this information in a system backup and restore it when you reinstall the game or set up another device.

Unity services

The game can connect to Unity services used for configuration and analytics. When those services are used, Unity may process technical information such as IP addresses and installation identifiers. The information processed depends on the Unity services in use. Unity describes its data handling, security practices, and privacy choices in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Data security

Game progress and preferences are stored locally in the app's storage, not on servers operated by the developer. Where Unity services process technical information, Unity applies its own security practices as described in its privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Game progress and settings remain in the game’s local storage until you clear that storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. Android manages any system backups separately. You can review and manage them through your device’s backup settings.

If you email the developer, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond to your request. We keep the correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request. You can contact us to ask about access to or deletion of your correspondence at gh16games@gmail.com.

For information about the retention and deletion of data processed by Unity, see the Unity privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

The game does not ask players to create an account or provide their name or email address to play. The Unity services described above may process technical information when used. Parents and guardians can contact us with questions about their child’s privacy or to request help with a privacy concern at gh16games@gmail.com.

Visiting this policy page

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit the page, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. See GitHub Pages documentation.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when relevant privacy practices change. The current policy will be available on this page, and the date at the top will show when it was last updated.
