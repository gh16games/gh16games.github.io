Privacy Policy for Puzzle Of Figures

Effective date: November 15, 2026.

Puzzle Of Figures is a free Android game developed by Hamlet Oniki Hovhannisyan. Its package name is com.GH16Games.PuzzleofFigures. For privacy questions, contact gh16games@gmail.com.

Playing the game

The game has no ads, in-app purchases, or player accounts. It stores your selected language, music and sound settings, and level progress in the app’s storage on your device so it can restore them when you play again.

Android releases and Unity services

An offline Android update (version code 10) is in preparation and has not been released. It is designed for offline play with Unity analytics and crash reporting turned off.

The published Android release, version 1.4.0 (version code 9), connects to Unity Config and Unity CDP, services used for configuration and analytics. Unity may process technical information such as IP addresses and installation identifiers when its services are used. The specific identifiers transmitted by this game have not been verified. Unity explains how it handles player and device information in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
